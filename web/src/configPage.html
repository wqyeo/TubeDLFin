    1 to 5. Keep it at 1 on a Raspberry Pi: merging and converting use the CPU.
    A cookies.txt file (Netscape format) from a browser signed in to the site. Needed for age-restricted or members-only videos, and when YouTube asks the server to "sign in to confirm you're not a bot". yt-dlp also writes refreshed cookies back to it. Leave blank for none.
